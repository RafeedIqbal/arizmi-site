## **Product purpose**

Arizmi BluePrint AI helps users turn a rough idea, workflow or product opportunity into a Product Requirements Document (PRD)-style product plan before development begins.

The app should feel premium, clean and simple. It is not a chatbot. It is a guided product scoping experience powered by AI.

## **Brand style**

Use this visual direction:

* Clean, premium tech interface  
* Warm off-white background: `#F7F5EF`  
* Card black: `#101313`  
* Arizmi teal light: `#03B6A3`  
* Arizmi teal mid: `#00AFA7`  
* Arizmi teal dark: `#019099`  
* Arizmi teal gradient: `#019099` to `#03B6A3`  
* Tech blue: `#2F8ED8`  
* Deep violet: `#6B4FD3`  
* Main font: Manrope  
* Metadata font: Space Mono

The experience should feel calm, clear and product-led, not gimmicky or overly AI-looking.
